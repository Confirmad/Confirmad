Questions people ask before booking

What if I've already sent a deposit?

We can still visit and tell you whether the apartment matches what you paid for, but we can't recover money that's already been sent. It's always better to book a check before paying anything.

Do you check apartments outside Madrid?

Not yet — right now we only visit properties within the city of Madrid.

What if the landlord won't let you in?

That goes straight into your report as a red flag. A landlord with a real apartment and nothing to hide rarely refuses a short visit.

Is this legal advice, or a lease review?

No. We confirm that a listing is genuine and matches what you've been shown. We're not lawyers, and we don't review contracts for legal validity.

How fast can you actually go?

Express checks are typically completed within 24–48 hours. Standard checks usually take 3–4 days, depending on availability that week.
