These terms cover your use of confirmad.es and any check you book through it. By booking a check, you agree to them.

2. What ConfirMad does

ConfirMad verifies a Madrid rental listing before you pay a deposit, and gives you a written result based on the plan you booked. All prices include VAT:

  • Online verification (€29.85) — we check the Land Registry (nota simple) to see if the advertiser matches the owner on record, reverse-search the photos, compare the price with others in the area, ask the advertiser for a video call or a visit, and review the terms you've been offered. Delivered in under 12 hours. Nobody visits the flat for this plan.
  • In-person visit (€59.99) — everything in Online verification, plus someone visits the address in person within 1 day of the advertiser confirming a time, and sends you a written report with dated photos.
  • Live visit (€99) — everything in In-person visit, plus a live video call from inside the apartment so you can see the rooms yourself and ask the advertiser questions in real time.

Prices are in euros, include VAT, and may change; the price shown when you book is the one that applies to your booking.

3. An obligation of means, not of result

ConfirMad provides a best-efforts verification service. We describe what we were able to check online, or what our visitor observed during an in-person visit, on a specific date and against the specific points listed below. We do not and cannot guarantee that the advertiser or landlord is legitimate, that they are the lawful owner of the property, or that any contract they later offer you is valid.

4. What we check, and what we don't

What we check: for Online verification — whether the person advertising the listing matches the owner shown on the Land Registry record (nota simple) we pull, whether the listing photos appear elsewhere online, how the price compares with others in the area, and whether the advertiser agrees to a video call or a visit. For an In-person or Live visit, we add: that the address exists and matches the listing, that the rooms match the photos you were sent, that someone is present to show the apartment, that the advertiser's name matches visually what they've told you, that no full payment is being demanded before a viewing, and what photos can't show you — the light, the noise at the time of the visit, the visible condition, the building entrance and the street.

What we don't check: legal ownership beyond matching the name on the Land Registry record we pull that day; whether any contract the advertiser later offers is legally valid or fair; hidden defects such as damp behind furniture, noise at other times of day, or heating that only fails in winter; and anything that changes after our check is complete — a different unit being offered, a new price, or a new payment method.

5. Consent to enter

We need the advertiser's consent to carry out an in-person or live visit. If they refuse entry, or despite reasonable coordination aren't present at the agreed time, that itself becomes a finding noted as a red flag in your report — see the refund policy below for what happens to your fee in that case.

6. Timing

The 1-day window for an In-person or Live visit starts counting once the advertiser confirms a time for the visit — not from when you book. If the advertiser doesn't respond within 48 hours, we'll let you know; you can then switch to Online verification instead, or follow the standard refund policy in section 10 above.

7. Live visit: the video call

For the Live visit, the video call is not recorded, and both the advertiser and any occupants present at the time must agree to it before it goes ahead. The call depends on the advertiser's cooperation and on having a usable internet connection at the property; if either isn't available, we'll do our best with what's possible and note it in your report.

ConfirMad is not a law firm and doesn't give legal advice. We don't review tenancy contracts or leases for legal validity, and our report is not a substitute for legal counsel.

We're not a party to your tenancy and we don't act as a rental agent. We don't negotiate the rental on your behalf, and we don't guarantee the landlord will rent to you or that the terms won't change after our check.

ConfirMad never receives, holds, or transfers your deposit or rent. Those payments happen directly between you and the landlord, entirely outside this site. The only payment you make to ConfirMad is the fee for the check itself, charged through Stripe when you book.

11. Your right of withdrawal

Under Spanish and EU consumer law, you normally have 14 days to withdraw from a service bought at a distance — like this one — without giving a reason, and get a full refund.

Because we start the check quickly, often within hours of booking, we ask you to expressly request that we begin before that 14-day period ends. Spanish law (article 103.a of the Consumer Protection Act, Real Decreto Legislativo 1/2007) allows this: once you've asked for immediate performance and the service has been fully carried out, you lose the right to withdraw and claim a refund for it. If you withdraw after we've started but before the check is complete, you pay only for the part of the service already provided, proportional to what's been done, not the full fee. You confirm all of this yourself, separately from agreeing to these terms, by checking the boxes when you book.

If we haven't started your check yet, you can still cancel and get a full refund by writing to contacto@confirmad.es. The standard EU/Spanish withdrawal form model is available here: modelo-desistimiento.html.
