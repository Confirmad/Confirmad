Cookie policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

Short version: we don't use tracking cookies

This site doesn't run Google Analytics or any similar analytics or advertising tool, and it doesn't set cookies of its own to track you across visits. That's why there's no cookie-consent banner here — there's nothing to ask your consent for.

What does touch third parties

A couple of things on this site involve outside services, and those services may set their own cookies under their own policies — not something we control:

  • Stripe. When you click "Pay now," you're taken to a checkout page hosted on stripe.com, which sets its own cookies to process your payment securely. See Stripe's cookie policy.
  • Google Fonts. The typefaces on this site are loaded from Google's font service, which involves a request to Google's servers (and shares your IP address with them) but doesn't set a cookie the way analytics tools do. See Google's privacy policy.

Local storage

Your browser may keep small bits of information locally to make the site work smoothly. This stays on your device and isn't used to track you elsewhere.

If this changes

If we ever add analytics or advertising cookies, we'll update this page and add a proper consent banner before any non-essential cookie is set.
